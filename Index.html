Foundational habits for a healthier lifestyle.
Note: This is educational and not medical advice.

💧 Hydration

Aim for 2-3 liters of water daily. Keep a bottle visible on your desk to encourage regular sips.

💤 Sleep

Target 7-9 hours. Consistency is crucial. Try to wake up and go to bed at the same time every day.

🚶 Daily Movement

Incorporate at least 30 minutes of walking or light movement, even on rest days to maintain mobility.

🧘 Mental Wellness

Practice 5 minutes of mindful breathing or meditation daily to lower baseline stress levels.

🧼 Hygiene

Maintain consistent personal hygiene routines to boost confidence and physical well-being.

📵 Digital Detox

Implement a 1-hour screen-free period before sleep to improve melatonin production.

Healthy Eating Basics

Focus on balanced meals, adequate protein, and whole foods.

  • Proteins: Chicken, tofu, lentils, eggs, Greek yogurt.
  • Carbs: Oats, brown rice, sweet potatoes, quinoa.
  • Fats: Avocados, nuts, olive oil, seeds.
  • Snacks: Apple with peanut butter, mixed nuts, carrots & hummus.
